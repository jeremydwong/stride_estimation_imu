How this was decided

Each of the 70 flagged bouts was rerun through the current pipeline (bout_sync_strides_steps on main afbf137, automatic snips from brock_s07_s08_auto_aligned.csv) and compared with the old result. Rules per problem type:

  • Walked back: fixed if the walk-back cut fired and no backward steps remain.
  • First step left out: fixed if the start moved back and gained a step, with no committed stepping left just before the new start.
  • Distance looked wrong: fixed if walked distance is now at or under target (+0.1 m). Most of her numbers were the old alignment estimate, which the rename to align_max_m already separated from walked distance.
  • Last step left out and unsure of the stop: no code change addresses these yet, so they stay manual unless the walk-back cut moved the end.

These are automatic checks. A bout marked fixed can still look wrong in its figure, so treat the figures as the final word.
