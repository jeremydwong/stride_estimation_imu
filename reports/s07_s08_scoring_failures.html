Fixes: one rule per error class [ranked by bouts covered]

Fix A · Walk back included: end overshoots into the return walk [cut the gait at the turn]

high confidence
covers 24 flagged (22 walk-back + 2 "watch video")clean bouts changed 1 / 72

The biggest class. The walker hands off, turns and walks back before anyone presses Stop, and the gait runs through the return. My stop check from last week made 6 of these worse: it went looking for a stop and found it after the walk back.

  • Detect: two or more steps going backwards along the walk's own direction (from footfall positions within the bout), or a gait lasting more than 1.4× what the distance should take (target / 1.25 m/s + 1 s). Catches 22 of 22 walk-backs; fires on 1 of 72 clean bouts.
  • Fix: end the gait at the landing of the farthest-forward footstep, and never extend a walk-back window looking for a stop.
  • Effect: gait times drop to plausible values (8.1 b2: 13.5 → 5.6 s; 45.2 b1: 12.4 → 5.3 s). Walked distance moves less than 0.25 m because it already stopped at the farthest footfall. One clean bout (23.2 b1) trims 6.6 → 5.2 s with the same distance.
  • Heading: this uses reversal within a bout, so it doesn't need the absolute heading to be right. The files do carry magnetometers and APDM's own orientation, so a session-wide heading (rep 1 one way, rep 2 the other) is available later if a case needs it.

Fix B · Missed first step: start search capped 1 s before the press [follow a chain of real steps back]

medium-high
covers 4 of 7 flagged first-step boutsclean bouts changed 2 / 72 (+1 step each)

Your hypothesis holds. The walker often starts before the Start press (the press wasn't a go cue), and the code never looks for the start more than 1 s before the press. In 12.1 b1 a full step sits at −1.4 s, drawn grey because it's outside the searched window.

  • Fix: if real steps (≥ 1.25 m/s, either foot) run back-to-back into the detected start with gaps ≤ 0.6 s, start the search at the first of them, up to 3 s back.
  • Effect: 45.1 b2 gains 2 steps and 1.6 m; 12.1 b1 and 34.1 b1 gain a step and about 0.3 m; 23.1 b2 gains a step.
  • Rejected: simply widening the cap to 3 s. That changed 12 of 72 clean bouts, pulling the start onto pre-walk activity (up to 2.9 s early, walked down by up to 1.9 m).
  • Not covered: 12.1 b2, 28.2 b2, 46.1 b2. Their first step is probably slower than 1.25 m/s, so it doesn't count as a real step.

Fix C · Missed last step: slow closing step below the step threshold [count steps ≥ 0.8 m/s at the end]

low · mostly cosmetic
covers 6 of 11 flagged last-step boutsclean bouts changed 12 / 72 (small)

After the last counted landing there's usually a slow closing shuffle (37.1 b2 is a clear example). It's under the 1.25 m/s that counts as a real step, so it isn't marked. Counting end steps down to 0.8 m/s marks it, but adds only 0.03–0.12 m. So this is mainly about what the figure shows, not lost distance.

  • It also adds a step to 12 clean bouts, again with tiny distance changes. Worth deciding whether the closing shuffle should count before shipping.

Fix D · Junk snip matched to a trial: no steps at all [unmatch bouts with < 2 footfalls]

high · tiny
covers 1 (37.2 b1, Hallee's "disaster")

The alignment matched a stretch of standing drift to 37.2 b1: 0 steps, 0.06 m walked. Any matched bout with fewer than 2 footfalls should become a missed bout automatically and go on the review list for G3/G4.

Under target · 1 (and every bout)

36.1 b1, ">1 m missing on 8 m". On her clean bouts, walked distance is short of target by a median 0.55–0.70 m at every distance, 2.5 m and 12 m alike. A constant offset like that points to where the walker stops relative to the mark (hand-off reach), not a missed step. Question for us: is the target measured to the feet?

Also out of the 70: 12 "distance looks wrong" notes ("4.3 / 2.5", "3.6 / 2.5", "8.3 / 8") were the old alignment number. Walked distance has replaced it, and those bouts now read at or under target. Already fixed.

Residual: one-offs after all fixes [8 bouts]

Last step, 5: 1.2 b1 · 3.2 b2 · 10.1 b1 · 38.1 b2 · 46.2 b1. First step, 3: 12.1 b2 · 28.2 b2 · 46.1 b2. These need a slower-step detector for starts and ends, or video. Covering them is what separates 85% from 95%.
